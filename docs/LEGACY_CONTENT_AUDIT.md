## 1. CV Do's and Don'ts

**Source URL:** `https://slimvic7.github.io/qwerty.github.io/service.html#content2-10`  
**Original Title:** Curriculum Vitae & Cover Letter tips  
**Topic/Category:** CV Guidance  
**Extracted File:** `/content/legacy/service-html.md`  

**Recommendation:** **Rewrite**  
**Reason for recommendation:** The core advice (don't use "Curriculum Vitae" as a header, use professional emails, don't include age/DOB by default) is sound and remains best practice. However, the advice suggesting to use a local contact's address if applying to a different city encourages misrepresentation, which violates QWERTY's new "truth before optimisation" mandate. The tone is also overly absolute in places.  
**Proposed Career Hub Category:** CV & Applications  
**Requires Current Verification:** Yes. The guidance on local addresses and absolute rules needs to be modernized and aligned with current recruitment and remote-work norms.
